# What's new in 5.5

**Course catalog replaced.** The earlier sample courses were removed. My Courses now holds the five USAII micro-credential courses, built from the course documents and answer keys:

1. AI Fluency for the Workplace
2. Prompting and Context Design
3. Agentic AI Foundations
4. RAG and Enterprise Knowledge Assistants
5. AI for Data Analysis, Excel, and BI

Each has Days 1 to 10 (concept, worked example, guided build, fill-in template, 3-question check), the 16-question final knowledge check, and the answer-key rationales. Every course starts as a **Draft** with **no price set**.

**Worked-example video slot.** On Days 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9 of every course, the worked example has one empty video item. Open the lesson in Course Builder, then upload the video and type its name. The transcript stays below as the accessible reference.

**Set course price.** Course details now has **Set course price (USD)**. Enter 0 for a free course, or a price under $50. A course cannot be published until its price is set; My Courses shows "Price not set" until then.

**Clean start.** Demo learner progress, discussion threads, and feedback tied to the old courses were removed. The three demo learner accounts remain with no enrollments. Existing data files are re-seeded on first start (data format 7).
